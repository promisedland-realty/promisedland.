Important Fraud & Scam Warning

⚠️
🚫

Never Pay in Advance

Do NOT transfer any token amount, advance, or booking fee to unknown individuals before physically visiting the property and verifying all documents.

📄

Verify Documents First

Always check original Patta, EC (Encumbrance Certificate), Sale Deed, and DTCP / CMDA approval before signing anything or making any payment.

💸

Beware of Fake Agents

Fraudsters may impersonate our agents. Promised Land never asks for money via UPI, WhatsApp, or cash without a proper written agreement.

📞

Verify Before You Trust
